# AI Knowledge Base - What It Does

Your application is a **comprehensive AI-powered knowledge management and chat platform** that serves as a central hub for users to interact with multiple AI models while organizing and managing their knowledge effectively.

## **Core Purpose**
It's essentially a **smart workspace** where users can:
- Chat with 20+ different AI models (OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, etc.)
- Upload and manage documents (PDFs, Word docs, text files, etc.)
- Organize conversations into projects and tag them
- Search through their entire knowledge base
- Generate images and audio using AI models

## **Key User Workflows**

1. **AI Conversations**: Users can start new chats with different AI models, ask questions, and get responses while maintaining conversation history

2. **Document Management**: Users upload documents that get processed and can be used as context for AI conversations - like having a personal knowledge base that AI can reference

3. **Knowledge Organization**: Users organize conversations into projects, add tags, and can search through everything they've discussed or uploaded

4. **Context-Aware AI**: The AI can reference uploaded documents and previous conversations to provide more relevant and informed responses

5. **Multi-Model Access**: Users can switch between different AI models (GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, etc.) depending on their needs

## **Target Users**
- **Researchers** who want to chat with AI about their documents
- **Students** organizing study materials and AI conversations
- **Professionals** managing project knowledge and AI assistance
- **Anyone** who wants a centralized place to interact with multiple AI models while keeping their conversations and documents organized

## **What Makes It Special**
Unlike just using ChatGPT or other AI tools, this gives users:
- **Persistence** - all conversations and documents are saved
- **Organization** - projects, tags, and searchable history
- **Context** - AI can reference uploaded documents
- **Choice** - multiple AI models in one place
- **Analytics** - track usage and see patterns

It's essentially a **personal AI research assistant** that remembers everything and helps users build a searchable knowledge base over time.

The application is currently running on Railway and appears to be a fully functional, production-ready system with both free-tier access and authenticated user capabilities.
